Question

With a single source of energy, light, and the means to eat, scavenge and infect, do food chains form by themselves? Rig A starts from a plant only, so any eating there has to evolve on its own; rig B starts from a plant that eats once in its life.

Setup

  • Energy is a stored reserve per organism, counted in bit flips. It enters only as light; tasks pay a little.
  • Five new instructions: photo, eat, infect, travel, look.
  • Day and night (a day is 32 rounds), seasons (a year is 8 days), temperature, fertile and barren land.
  • Dead bodies stay where they fall, can be eaten, and rot.

Why it ended

While it ran, seven independent readers reviewed the code and two more checked every finding. Nineteen defects were confirmed, four of them in the rules: energy could be made from nothing by eating cheap children, reserves could go below zero, look counted dead bodies as neighbours, and rounds could stretch until a region stood still at one hour of the day. The run was stopped and kept, and the sun world started again from its founders.
