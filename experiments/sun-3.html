Question

No founder can sense, guard or feel. Do these abilities appear by themselves, do they spread, and what do they do to eating? The second start is the yardstick: the same two founders in the same world, under the rules before these.

The rules

Rules 5 add three instructions to the sun world. sense reads how bright the light is, guard buys a parry that turns one bite away, and feel tells whether something was bitten close by. Harvesting is a skill: every organism is born with its own, set by how thriftily its mother built it. Light is the only source of energy: a correct answer to a task is paid out of the light of its region, so nothing is paid in the dark.

The three instructions are one bit flip away from photo and eat. No founder has them, so the abilities have to appear by mutation.

  • Guarding was used. Of the bites that reached a living body in the last round, 19.6% were turned away on rig A and 1.6% on rig B. Over the whole run it was 14.5% and 1.2%.
  • Eating appeared on rig A, which started from a plant only: at the stop 11.3% of its organisms were plants that also eat. On rig B, which started from a grazer, it was 50.2%.

Why it ended

The owner ordered rules 6 to be started as soon as their checks passed. This start was stopped cleanly to make room for them, and its data is kept under the tag sun-v3. The fourth start follows it.
