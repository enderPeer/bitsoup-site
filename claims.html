Claims

What would count as a virus, a parasite, a predator that lasts, speech that carries information? Each claim has its test and its threshold written down before the tools that test it existed, and before anyone looked at what they would print. A verdict is per rig and per start, and "not found" is only said after the detector has found a planted example.

How a genome is put to the test (alone, beside a host, with a byte knocked out) and what the tests found on the second start: the assay and the sandbox.
