Data

Everything the pages show, as files. They are rebuilt with the site; a row never changes once its round is over, except the last one. A long run is thinned to a row for every few minutes, as its line in the table says; the rows that stay are not changed. In an experiment's files "rigs" holds one entry for each part of the world that counts its own rounds: a rig, or, where every device runs a band of its own, a device.

Fields of an experiment
