  3. Nov 11 22:05 UTCdeploy
    Travellers deflated and let go after sending; both nodes started again (6937102)

    The cause of both crashes: since 9044974 the batches of travellers were the engine's records, not deflated. At dawn one round's travellers came to about 10 GB on rig A, and the node's main process held them two to three times over. The fix: the workers deflate the batches (5.1 times smaller, measured), and the main process lets go of its copy of the arrivals as soon as they are sent. The cards get a 384 KB arena for leavers, because the dawn give-backs (up to 13,730 regions in a round on one card) all came from the 128 KB arena running full; their slots are reduced to fit, to 57,000 and 25,000 regions on rig A and 25,000 on each of rig B's three cards. Checked with the world's tests, the link journal's checks and the node check on the RTX 4090 with the new arena. Both rigs' workers stored every region when their main processes died (all 283,527 of rig B's region files were rewritten), so the start reads them all first, as after any unclean stop, and then goes on: little or nothing of the world is lost. RUNTIME watches both rigs through the next dawn.
