  3. Nov 15 14:21 UTCfinding
    Both rigs out of reach for 15 minutes: by their own readings they ran on, and the fault lay on the way to them

    From 13:48 to 14:02 UTC on 15 November the control PC reached neither rig, and the alarm said of both that they do not answer. The samplers on the rigs kept writing a reading a minute meanwhile, and those readings arrived afterwards. They show both machines running on as before: the same load on processors and cards, the cards at 48 to 64 degrees, not one bus error counted, and each rig's network port receiving and sending about one to two megabytes in nearly every minute, as in the hours before, which is the two rigs talking to each other. So neither rig A's network chip nor rig B's chipset link failed this time, as they had on 12 November. What failed lay between the control PC and the two rigs, and the readings do not say which piece. In the last minute before the silence a copy of 135 MB ran from rig A to the PC over that path and arrived whole. Both machines were restarted by hand at about 13:58 UTC, after ten minutes of silence, and answered again from 14:02. Whether the restart or something else brought the path back is not known. The restart was hard: each sampler's file of the day ended in a torn line, which was cut off before the samplers were started again at 14:14 UTC, so between 13:57 and 14:14 the machines' readings are five minutes apart instead of one. While the rigs were not read, the overview called the halted fifth start "Running now" and showed the speed of its last round. That is mended: a world that stood still when it was last read stays halted, and its page says that it is not being read.
